About us

Project BloodLine™ is a San Antonio nonprofit that teaches people how to stop severe bleeding, for free, in English and Spanish.

Why we exist

There are plenty of STOP THE BLEED® classes in San Antonio, but most are paid, tied to specific programs, and taught only in English. That leaves out a lot of people: Spanish-speaking families, rural communities and neighborhoods where nobody ever comes to teach this.

Our mission is simple: give everyone the skills and confidence to act when someone is bleeding, no matter their background, their language or what they can pay.

How it started

Project BloodLine was founded by Andre Aguirre, a UTSA student and certified instructor, and filed as a Texas nonprofit in August 2025. We taught our first class at UTSA on September 24, 2025, to four students.

Some of those first trainees came back the next week to help teach. Since then, nine former trainees have returned as assistants or instructors, and more than 175 people have been certified.

How we work

  • Every class is free for the people taking it.
  • We teach the Stop the Bleed Coalition's course and use real tourniquets and wound trainers.
  • We teach in English or Spanish.
  • We survey every class before and after, anonymously, and use the answers to improve. See the results.
