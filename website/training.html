The training

One free, hands-on class. You leave knowing how to stop a life-threatening bleed, with a certificate to prove it.

How a class works

  1. A short anonymous survey, so we know where the group is starting from.
  2. A short presentation on spotting life-threatening bleeding and the STOP THE BLEED® ABCs: Alert, Bleeding, Compress.
  3. Hands-on practice. Everyone applies direct pressure, packs a wound trainer and puts on a real tourniquet, with an instructor checking their work.
  4. A second short survey, then your certificate arrives by email.

It usually takes about an hour.

Who it's for

Anyone. You don't need medical experience, and most of our trainees have none. We've taught student organizations, classes and community members, and we'll come to schools, churches, workplaces and community centers around San Antonio.

What we bring and what you bring

We bring the instructors, tourniquets, gauze and wound trainers. You bring a room with tables and the people. A projector helps, but we can teach without one.

Certificate

Everyone who completes the class gets a Stop the Bleed Coalition course completion certificate by email. Nobody has failed one of our classes yet.
