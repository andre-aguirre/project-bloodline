What you'll learn

We teach the Stop the Bleed Coalition's course, built around the STOP THE BLEED® ABCs. You'll practice on wound trainers with real tourniquets and gauze, so the first time you do it isn't on a real person.

  1. A

    Alert

    Call 911 and make sure the scene is safe before you step in. You can't help anyone if you get hurt too.

  2. B

    Bleeding

    Find where it's coming from and tell when it's life-threatening: blood that's spurting, pooling or soaking through clothes.

  3. C

    Compress

    Stop it. Hard direct pressure, packing the wound with gauze (or a clean shirt if that's all you've got), and putting a tourniquet on an arm or leg.

Bring a training to your group

It's free. Send us an email and we'll set up a date. We bring the instructors, tourniquets and wound trainers.

So far we've mostly trained UTSA students, but we'll come to schools, churches, workplaces and community centers around San Antonio. We especially want to reach Spanish-speaking and rural communities, where these classes almost never happen.

Who we are

Project BloodLine™ is a San Antonio 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded by a UTSA student. Plenty of STOP THE BLEED® classes exist in town, but most are paid, limited to certain programs, and taught only in English. We want to go where they don't.
